03 / What we found + what we would fix

MAKE IT EASIER
TO CHOOSE YOU.One issue. One clear fix.

Each finding below explains what we saw, why it could get in a customer’s way, and the practical change we would make.

Finding 01 / 06

Google’s booking button reaches an error page.

What we found

The booking button on Google Maps points to the retired Elk Grove domain. That exact page returned “404 Not Found,” which means the page could not be found. The current homepage also links to the old domain.

Why this can lose inquiries

This is a high-intent moment: a patient has already chosen to book. An error page leaves them without the next step they expected.

Our fix

Update the Google booking link first. Replace old website links and redirect old pages where the practice controls them. Check the route from Google through to a working appointment request.

Proof: Google booking destination ↗
Finding 02 / 06

The office tour says “Coming Soon.”

What we found

The linked “Tour Our Office” page does not yet show the office. It displays a “Coming Soon” message.

Why this can lose inquiries

A patient looking for reassurance about the space gets another unanswered question. Real rooms and a familiar face can help them feel ready to call.

Our fix

Film a short walk-through with real room photos. Explain arrival, parking and what happens at the first appointment, then use useful clips in local Facebook and Instagram ads.

Proof: Office-tour page ↗
Finding 03 / 06

Patients are requesting a time, not booking it.

What we found

The form asks for a preferred date and time, and staff confirm later. The homepage says to expect a reply in one to two business days.

Why this can lose inquiries

If someone thinks their appointment is already confirmed, the later callback can be confusing. Someone needing a quicker answer may not know which route to use.

Our fix

Use “Request an appointment” consistently. Show the reply time beside the form and make the phone option clear. Agree how requests reach the team and how patients receive confirmation.

Proof: Appointment request page ↗
Finding 04 / 06

A treatment heading still reads like an edit note.

What we found

The homepage’s All-on-4 card is titled “Add All On 4.” That is the wording a visitor sees before opening the treatment page.

Why this can lose inquiries

Small unfinished details can make a patient question how current the page is, especially when they are comparing practices for a major treatment.

Our fix

Replace the heading with a clear patient-facing title. Check the other service cards and explain the difference between individual implants and the practice’s All-on-4 treatment route.

Proof: Homepage service cards ↗
Finding 05 / 06

The smile photos need a little explanation.

What we found

The gallery shows before-and-after images, but the reviewed page does not explain each case’s procedure, timeline or treating clinician.

Why this can lose inquiries

Photos alone cannot tell a patient whether a story relates to their situation. Without context, useful proof can leave them guessing or expecting the same result.

Our fix

Add approved, permission-cleared captions with the treatment, timeline and individual context. Film a clinician explaining a suitable case, including relevant limitations and no promise of an identical result.

Proof: Smile Gallery ↗
Finding 06 / 06

The first mobile screen could arrive sooner.

What we found

Google’s October 20 mobile lab test scored 62/100. The largest visible element appeared after 7.4 seconds on an emulated slow-4G connection. The report had no real-user dataset, so it does not describe every patient visit.

Why this can lose inquiries

A patient clicking an ad on a phone may leave before the useful information appears. A slower first screen makes the practice work harder to earn that next click.

Our fix

Reduce heavy images and files that delay the first screen, and improve reuse of downloaded files. Also fix the report’s missing button names and image descriptions so the pages are easier to use with assistive tools.

Proof: Mobile PageSpeed report ↗
