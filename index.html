What we found / 1 of 3

FINDINGS. Worth fixing.

01

Old links block ready patients.

Your homepage points to the old domain; Google’s booking URL returned 404. Patients who’ve chosen you can lose the request path. October 20 check; the main website works. See your Google profile ↗

02

Your implant proof needs context.

The gallery lacks case-by-case procedure, timing and treating-clinician details. Implant shoppers cannot judge how those examples relate to their needs, leaving questions unanswered before they request a consultation. See the gallery ↗

03

The office tour is unfinished.

The dedicated tour page says Coming Soon. Anxious patients miss a chance to see the rooms and feel comfortable enough to request a visit. Office photos exist elsewhere. See the office tour ↗

What we found / 2 of 3

FINDINGS. Worth fixing.

04

Polish the implant message.

The homepage heading reads Add All On 4. A visible editing mistake beside major treatment can distract from your doctors’ expertise when patients are deciding whether to trust the practice. See the service heading ↗

05

Patients wait for the next step.

Appointment requests need staff confirmation; the homepage promises 1–2 business days. Patients wanting a quick answer may contact another practice. Actual response times were not measured. See the request page ↗
